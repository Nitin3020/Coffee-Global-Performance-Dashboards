# ☕ Coffee Global Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Short Description

The Coffee Global Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze coffee sales performance across products, customers, regions, and countries. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor revenue trends, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

| Technology    | Purpose                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Power BI      | Data Visualization & Dashboard Development |
| Power Query   | Data Cleaning & Transformation             |
| DAX           | KPI Calculations & Data Modeling           |
| Excel/CSV     | Data Source                                |
| Data Analysis | Business Intelligence & Reporting          |

---

## 📂 Data Source

The dataset contains coffee sales transactions including:

* Sales Revenue
* Quantity Sold
* Coffee Types & Products
* Customer Segments
* Customer Ratings
* Country & Regional Data
* Monthly & Yearly Sales Records

# 💼 Business Problem

Coffee businesses operating across multiple locations often struggle to:

* Track overall sales performance efficiently.
* Identify their most profitable products.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Compare performance across countries and regions.
* Monitor sales trends over time.

Without a centralized reporting solution, decision-making becomes slow and less effective.

---

# 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was developed to:

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Analyze sales trends and growth opportunities.
* Identify top-performing coffee products.
* Understand customer segment contributions.
* Compare regional and country-wise sales.
* Support strategic decision-making through data-driven insights.

# 📈 Business Impact & Insights

### Key Insights

* Generated insights from **₹7.56M+ total sales revenue** and **20,861 transactions**.
* Mocha and Macchiato emerged as the highest revenue-generating coffee categories.
* France and USA contributed significantly to overall sales performance.
* Premium and New Customers represented the largest share of revenue contribution.
* Average customer rating remained close to **4.0**, indicating positive customer satisfaction.

### Business Impact

✅ Improved visibility into sales performance across countries and regions.

✅ Enabled identification of top-performing products and customer segments.

✅ Supported inventory and product strategy decisions.

✅ Helped uncover seasonal sales patterns for better forecasting.

✅ Facilitated data-driven decision-making through interactive business intelligence reporting.
