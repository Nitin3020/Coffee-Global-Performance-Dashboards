## 📖 Project Overview

The **Coffee Global Performance Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence solution developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and monitor global coffee sales performance.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, regional contributions, and yearly growth patterns. It enables decision-makers to quickly identify business opportunities, improve operational efficiency, and drive data-driven strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Coffee retailers operating across multiple countries often face challenges in:

* Tracking sales performance across regions.
* Understanding customer purchasing behavior.
* Identifying top-performing products.
* Monitoring yearly and monthly revenue trends.
* Recognizing weak-performing markets and customer segments.
* Making informed decisions using fragmented reports.

Without centralized reporting, businesses struggle to uncover valuable insights and growth opportunities.

---

## 🚀 Project Goal

The primary objective of this dashboard is to:

✅ Monitor overall business performance

✅ Analyze product-wise sales contribution

✅ Track monthly and yearly sales trends

✅ Understand customer segmentation

✅ Evaluate regional and country-level performance

✅ Support strategic business decision-making
